# Oonava motion video prompts

How to use: paste the **Brand brief** first, then one video prompt at a time. Generate the videos one by one, in order.

---

## Brand brief (paste this first, once)

You are making short motion-graphic sales videos for **Oonava**, an AI automation and software agency serving small and growing businesses in the UK and Europe. Tagline: **"Automate. Integrate. Build."**

Style rules for every video:
- **Look:** clean, premium, modern SaaS motion design. Flat UI mockups, smooth easing, kinetic typography. No stock-footage people, no robots, no glowing AI brains.
- **Colours:** background #03030F (near-black) or #FFFFFF. Accent electric blue #26A0F8, deep blue #0B6FC0. Light greys #F3F3F3 / #E4E4E7. Status colours used sparingly: green #28C840 (done), red #C8322F (hot / missed).
- **Type:** Urbanist (or a similar geometric sans), bold headlines, tight letter-spacing. At most 7 words on screen at once.
- **Logo:** lowercase "oonava" with a blue full stop: **oonava.**
- **Motion:** objects slide up and fade in (ease-out), UI cards build step by step, a blue line "draws" between connected tools. Nothing spins for no reason.
- **Sound:** modern minimal beat, soft UI clicks and whooshes on each step, no cheesy stingers.
- **Voiceover:** confident, warm, plain English (British), never hype. Talk about the viewer's business, not about AI.
- **Format:** make a 9:16 vertical cut (Reels, TikTok, Shorts, WhatsApp status) and a 16:9 cut (website, sales calls, LinkedIn). Burned-in captions on both.
- **Every video:** hook in the first 2 seconds, one clear problem, show the system working, one outcome, end on the CTA card.
- **End card (all videos):** "oonava." logo, "Automate. Integrate. Build.", "Book a free 30-min mapping call", "oonava.com".
- **Honesty:** these are concept demos. Don't show fake client logos, fake testimonials or invented statistics. The only numbers allowed are the ones written in each prompt.

## Video 1: Agency intro: "Your business, running itself" (45–60s)

**Hook (0–3s):** Black screen. A phone buzzes: "New enquiry, 21:47, Sunday." On-screen text: **"Who's replying to this?"** Beat. **"Nobody. Until Monday."**

**Problem (3–12s):** Quick cuts of mess. An inbox stacking to 47 unread, sticky notes, a spreadsheet with red cells, a calendar clash. VO: "Most businesses don't lose customers to competitors. They lose them to slow replies, forgotten follow-ups and admin nobody has time for."

**Turn (12–15s):** Everything freezes. The blue full stop from the logo drops in and "cleans" the screen. Text: **"What if it just… ran?"**

**Show (15–40s):** Three short beats, each labelled:
1. **Automate.** An enquiry arrives and is answered in seconds with a personalised reply.
2. **Integrate.** CRM, calendar, email and WhatsApp icons join up with drawn blue lines, and data flows between them.
3. **Build.** A custom dashboard assembles itself card by card.

VO: "Oonava builds the systems behind your business: automations that reply, follow up and update everything for you, connected to the tools you already use."

**Outcome (40–50s):** Text stack: **"Faster replies. No lost leads. Less admin."** VO: "Your team stops chasing and starts closing."

**CTA (50–60s):** End card. VO: "Book a free 30-minute mapping call. We'll show you exactly what to automate first."

## Video 2: Lead Conversion System: "The 38-second reply" (30–45s)

**Hook (0–3s):** Big timer counting up from 0:00. Text: **"How fast do you reply to a new lead?"** It hits 38s and stops on green: **"We reply in 38 seconds."** *(Concept demo figure.)*

**Problem (3–10s):** Split screen. Left: "Your competitor", the enquiry is still unread and a clock reads "Mon 09:12". Right: the same lead books a viewing somewhere else. VO: "Leads go cold in minutes, not days. Whoever replies first usually wins."

**Show (10–32s):** Animate the inbox UI:
- An enquiry lands: "Sarah Mitchell, Rightmove: 3-bed apartment, Manchester, ~£500k, moving in 3 months."
- The AI pulls out fields one by one into cards: Type, Area, Budget, Timeline, Intent.
- A score badge flips from "Scoring…" to red **"HOT · 92"**.
- A personalised reply types itself and sends: "Would Tuesday 14:00 or Wednesday 17:30 suit for a viewing?"
- Three chips pop in: "✓ Viewing booked · Tue 14:00", "CRM updated", "Agent notified".

VO: "The moment an enquiry arrives, it's read, scored and answered. The hot ones are booked straight into your calendar, any time of day."

**Outcome (32–40s):** Text: **"Every lead answered. Even at 21:47 on a Sunday."**

**CTA:** End card. Extra line: "See it on your own enquiries."

## Video 4: Operations System: "One booking. Six jobs. Zero clicks." (30–40s)

**Problem (3–10s):** A frazzled checklist: update CRM, add to calendar, send confirmation, schedule reminder, brief the agent, save record. Ticks appear slowly, one is missed and flashes red. VO: "Every booking sets off a chain of admin. Miss one step and the client notices."

**Show (10–30s):** Dark workflow UI titled "Workflow · viewing booked". From a blue "Trigger: Booking" node, six steps light up in sequence with a drawn blue line and green status text:
CRM → "Opportunity updated", Calendar → "Event created", Email → "Confirmation sent", WhatsApp → "Reminder scheduled", Agent → "Briefing delivered", Database → "Record saved".
Status pill turns green: **"Completed · 1.8s"**.

VO: "Oonava connects your tools so one action triggers everything that should follow, correctly and in seconds."

**Outcome:** Text: **"Your team does the work only humans can do."**

**CTA:** End card.
