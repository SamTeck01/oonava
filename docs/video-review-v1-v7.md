# Review of Oonava videos V1–V7 (both formats)

Paste this whole file to the video-generating Claude. Fix the global issues first; they affect every video.

---

## Overall verdict

Strong base. The brand is consistent: black/white, electric blue, Urbanist-style type, the blue full stop. The UI mockups look premium and every video follows hook → problem → system → outcome → CTA. The hooks are good. The main problems are finishing issues: a cursor left in shot, end cards that run far too long, a few broken transitions, and clipped text. Fix the global list and these are ready to post.

---

## Global fixes (apply to all 14 files)

1. **Remove the mouse cursor.** A pointer is visible on the end card of V1 (16:9), V2, V3, V4, V5, V6 and V7, often hovering on the "Book a free 30-min mapping call" button. It looks like a screen recording. Re-render with the cursor hidden.
2. **Shorten the end card to 3–4 seconds.** At the moment the end card holds for about 6–10 seconds, roughly 25–35% of each video. On Reels and TikTok, people swipe away during a static card and the video counts as "not finished", which hurts reach. Cut it to about 3.5s and end on a crisp frame, or loop back to the hook for vertical.
3. **No blur transitions on text.** Several caption and headline swaps blur and smear mid-word: V1 "Stop chasing / Start…", V5 "Fewer no-shows", V6 "Clear scope / Fixed price / Ongoing support", V7 "What does automation cost?". Use the brand motion instead: slide up and fade, ease-out, no blur.
4. **Keep all text inside the safe area.** Leave 8% margins on 16:9, and on 9:16 keep text at least 120px from the left and right edges and clear of the bottom 20%, where Instagram and TikTok put their buttons. Clipped examples are listed under each video below.
5. **Captions: show the whole line clearly.** The word-by-word highlight leaves the unspoken words very faint grey (e.g. "not days.", "usually wins."). Raise the unspoken words to at least 60% opacity so the line is readable on mute. Most people watch with sound off.
6. **Use the 9:16 frame properly.** In the vertical versions the content sits in a small band in the middle, with large empty areas above and below. Scale the UI mockups and headlines up by about 30–40% so they fill the phone screen. Headline text should be at least about 72px on 1080×1920.
7. **Hook in the first frame.** The first frame is what shows as the thumbnail and in the feed before playback. Make sure frame 0 already shows the hook text, not an empty or fading-in screen.
8. **Audio:** levels are consistent across all videos (about −16 dB average), which is good. Add a subtle UI click or whoosh on each step reveal if there isn't one, and duck the music under the voiceover.

## Per-video notes

### V1: Agency intro (44s)
- **Hook ("21:47 … Who's replying to this? Nobody. Until Monday.")** is the strongest hook in the set. Keep it.
- The problem section is good. In 16:9, the inbox mockup is cut off at the top of the frame; move it down inside the safe area.
- The Automate / Integrate / Build sequence works. "Integrate" leaves a lot of empty white space on the right in 9:16; centre the diagram.
- "Faster replies. / Stop chasing." The text blurs and smears; use slide-up (global fix 3).
- At 44s it's the longest. Trim it to about 35s: speed up the "Stop chasing → Start closing" beat and cut the end card to 3.5s.

### V2: The 38-second reply (33s)
- The timer hook is great. Keep the green "38 seconds" moment and the "Concept demo" label.
- "Your competitor / Mon 09:12" split works.
- Two captions in a row are near-duplicates ("Whoever replies first, usually wins." appears twice); remove the repeat.
- The "Every lead answered" headline blurs in. Use the clean slide-up instead.
- The end card holds about 9s; cut it to 3.5s.

### V3: Follow-Up Engine (31s)
- The hook "Most leads say no. Because nobody asked twice." is strong.
- The spreadsheet of "No reply" rows works well in dark mode.
- The kanban section is good, but the cards are tiny in 9:16. Scale the board up so the names are readable on a phone.
- The "Emma replied" notification is the payoff. Hold it about 0.5s longer and add a little pop.
- The end card has the cursor sitting on the button; remove it.

### V4: Operations System (28.5s)
- The hook "One booking creates 6 jobs. Who's doing them?" is clear and punchy.
- The missed "Schedule reminder" in red is a great moment.
- The workflow lights up nicely, and "Completed · 1.8s" is a good payoff.
- The transition from the dark workflow to the white "Your team does the work only humans can do" is a hard cut that flashes. Use a quick fade or a blue wipe.
- The "humans" script font sits awkwardly on the line. Align its baseline with the sans text.
- Remove the cursor and shorten the end card.

### V5: Real estate (27s)
- The hook "Six enquiries. You're on a viewing." is very relevant to agents, and the "Lost to another agent" red tag is good.
- There are thin black vertical lines at the edges of some frames during the transitions between scenes. These look like rendering seams; fix them.
- The "Offer stage" chip has a blue dot overlapping its text. Clean up that animation.
- The bullet outcome "More viewings. Fewer no-shows. No lost leads." blurs in (global fix 3).
- "For estate & letting agents." on the end card is good; keep it.

### V6: How we work (41.5s)
- The hook "Scared of a tech project going wrong?" is good.
- The 5-step timeline (Map → Design → Build → Launch → Support) is clear, but too slow. Each step holds about 4s. Cut each to about 2.5s and get the video to about 30s.
- Between Launch and Support the whole timeline blurs out. Remove that blur.
- **Clipped text:** "Ongoing support" is cut off on the left edge ("ngoing"). Fix the safe area.
- The "Fixed price · Locked" card is a great trust visual. Make it bigger.

### V7: Pricing (25s)
- **Broken transition:** a big plain blue circle covers the hook text "What does automation cost?" for about a second. It looks like a failed wipe or mask. Fix or remove it; it's the most visible bug in the set.
- **Clipped text (9:16):** "Three ways to work with us." runs off both edges in several frames ("hree ways…", "…with us" cut). Reduce the font size or wrap it onto two lines.
- In 9:16 the pricing cards overflow the right edge when "Most popular" is highlighted. Keep the cards inside the frame.
- The prices match the website (€1,500 + €199, €4,500 + €499, from €10,000 + from €999).
- The hook is weak compared with the others. Consider "Less than one admin hire." or "What if your admin cost €199 a month?" instead of "What does automation cost?".
